Simple From Day One

Everything a great music player should do.

Other apps try to sell you a subscription every time you open them. Elysonic stays focused on the records you already own, from a small folder of favorite CDs to a lifetime collection.

Your Apple Music Library

Plays the songs you own in Apple Music

Already have ripped CDs or purchased songs in Apple Music? Point Elysonic right at that folder with no duplicate copies. It plays the music you bought or ripped (not rented Apple Music streams), never edits, moves, or deletes those files, and brings over your playlists and play counts in one step. If you ever edit an album's details, Elysonic saves a copy in a folder you choose so Apple Music stays untouched.

Built for Real Collections

Your folders, your drives, your files

Plays the music files you already have, including MP3, FLAC, ALAC, AAC, and WAV. Search your whole library in a split second, unplug an external drive without losing your records, and upgrade old files to cleaner copies while keeping your favorites and playlists.

No Mistakes

Bring your own APIs.
Unlock everything else.

Every online lookup service, music database, and AI feature below starts turned off. Elysonic holds zero API keys of its own, collects zero telemetry, and runs no servers. The direct Mac download checks for app updates automatically (you can turn that off in Settings). Open Settings to turn on only the services you want and plug in your own free accounts, personal keys, or local AI.

1 · Flip a Switch Free · No key needed

Community music databases

Start off until you turn them on in Settings › Online services and approve what leaves your Mac.

  • MusicBrainz & Cover Art Archive Release edition checks, missing song audits, discography gaps, Music Tree, Five Degrees, and missing album covers.
  • Wikipedia & Wikidata Artist biographies, band member histories, and Wikimedia Commons photo pickers.
  • LRCLIB Automatic synced lyrics lookup for songs without embedded lyrics, plus optional sharing of your timed edits.
2 · Provide Your Own Key Your free personal key · Keychain

Bring your own API keys

Create your own free personal key with the service and paste it into Settings. Keys stay safely in your Mac's Keychain.

  • Discogs Deep pressing and edition lookup plus high-resolution release cover candidates.
  • AcoustID Recognize mystery songs by how they sound. Computes a fingerprint locally and never uploads audio.
  • fanart.tv Extra high-resolution album covers and artist photography.
  • Spotify & Callasso Read-only Spotify playlist import (using your own free Spotify developer Client ID). Nearby concerts via Callasso, an app from the makers of Elysonic, launching soon.
3 · Provide Your Own AI Local ($0) or Cloud API Key

Bring your own AI & MCP

Run open models locally for $0, or paste your own cloud API key (billed directly by your provider within daily caps you set).

  • Ollama & LM Studio (Local) Run local models right on your Mac with zero per-token bills and full privacy.
  • Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok & Mistral Paste your own API key for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, or Mistral, assign different models by job, set fallback order, and cap daily background use.
  • Local Vocal & Whisper Models + MCP Optional on-device lyric alignment models (about 2 GB), plus a local MCP server to connect Claude Desktop and other AI clients.
Why does bringing your own APIs matter? Because we never sit in the middle. Elysonic runs no proxy servers, charges no subscription, marks up zero AI tokens, and never touches your keys outside your Keychain. You unlock as much power as you want, and you still pay us $0.

Unlocked · Album Journeys

Hear the story behind every era you own.

Requires Full Discography MusicBrainz Wikipedia Your Own AI

Enable online lookup and connect your own local or cloud AI, and Album Journeys turns a complete studio discography into an interactive, narrated documentary played straight from your own files.

Journeys build quietly in the background and appear once every chapter is finished. Each chapter pairs source-backed writing, credits, related events, and band photography with a synchronized timeline of narration and songs from your library. Local AI costs $0; cloud AI bills to your own provider account within the daily cap you set. (Apple Music read-only users pick a folder of their own for journeys.)

  • Earned by owning the records. Browse finished journeys, see which ones are "Coming soon" as they build in the background, and spot artists on the "Almost there" shelf once you own at least three-quarters of their studio albums.
  • Shareable .elyjourney files. Export a finished journey with stories, credits, and playback cues. Friends open it as a separate telling powered by their own local music library.
  • Multi-model compare tellings. Assign different assistants across steps, keep source citations attached, and compare tellings across assistants you configured.

Playback, Listening Room & Radio

Out of the box
  • Full-stage Listening Room with Cover, Visualizer & Up Next modes

  • Keep with next & Continuous mix runs on shuffle

Audio Engine, EQ & Visualizers

Out of the box
