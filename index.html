Simple From Day One

Everything a great music player should do.

Other apps try to sell you a subscription every time you open them. Elysonic stays focused on the music you already own, whether you have fifty CDs or five thousand albums.

Your Apple Music Library

Plays the songs you own in Apple Music

Already have ripped CDs or purchased songs in Apple Music? Point Elysonic right at that folder with no duplicate copies. It plays the music you bought or ripped (not rented Apple Music streams), never edits, moves, or deletes those files, and brings over your playlists and play counts in one step.

Built for Real Collections

Your folders, your drives, your files

Plays FLAC, ALAC, MP3, AAC, WAV, and more. Search your whole library in a split second, unplug an external drive without losing your records, and upgrade old files to cleaner copies while keeping your favorites and playlists.

No Mistakes

Never lose your Up Next list

Accidentally wiped out an afternoon's queue with one wrong click? Elysonic keeps your last ten queues so you can jump right back, and Undo reverses mistakes across the app.

Album Craft

Plays albums the way they were made

Live albums and medleys flow without awkward pauses between tracks. Keep paired songs glued together even on shuffle, skip long dead silence, set a sleep timer at night, or stop cleanly when the album ends.

Even Volume

No more surprise loud songs

Old CDs are quiet. New records are loud. Even Volume keeps every song at a comfortable level automatically, while keeping quiet intros and big choruses natural when you listen to an album straight through.

Easy to Tune

Simple controls, deep when you want it

Start with Bass, Middle, and Treble. A full 10-band equalizer, headphone comfort blend, and saved speaker or headphone setups are built right in whenever you want finer control.

Listening Room & Mini Player

Big screen or tucked in a corner

Open the full-screen Listening Room for big album art and live visuals, pop out the compact Mini Player while you work, or switch on Karaoke Mode to lower vocals and sing along with saved lyrics.

Rediscover & Keep It Safe

Fall back in love with the albums you forgot.

Hear overlooked records again with personal stations made from your own library, and tidy up messy folders without worrying about breaking a single file.

Library Stations

Start a station from any song or artist

Click ⋯ on any song or artist in your library and choose Start station to turn your own collection into a continuous mix. Slide the Familiar-to-Discover dial right inside Up Next, and use thumbs up or down to steer what plays next.

Balanced Mix
Familiar Balanced Deep Cuts

Blends favorite songs near your starting song with unplayed album cuts from related genres. Downvoted songs move to Played less.

Smart playlists

Build playlists that update themselves from simple rules, or bring in standard playlist files anytime.

Rediscover shelves

Surfaces great albums sitting in your library that you haven't played in years.

Keep It Tidy

Spot duplicates & messy names

Flagged gathers duplicate songs, artists spelled two different ways, moved files, and broken tracks in one place. Listen to copies side by side and Undo fixes anytime.

Safe Edits & Covers

Fix details & drop in covers

Drag any cover picture onto an album, give every disc in a box set its own artwork, or join split albums. Your original music files stay untouched unless you choose to update them.

Daily Backups

Automatic backups, no cloud bill

Your library, playlists, listening history, and chosen covers are backed up automatically every day (7 copies kept), with a safety copy created before any restore.

Free Radio

Live stations whenever you're online

Bring your own APIs.
Unlock everything else.

Every online lookup service, music database, and AI feature below starts turned off. Elysonic holds zero API keys of its own, runs no servers, and never phones home behind your back. Open Settings, turn on only the services you want, and plug in your own free accounts, personal API keys, or local AI.

1 · Flip a Switch Free · No API key needed

Community music databases

Start off until you turn them on in Settings › Online services and approve what leaves your computer.

  • MusicBrainz & Cover Art Archive Release edition checks, missing track audits, discography gaps, Music Tree, Five Degrees, and missing album covers.
  • Wikipedia & Wikidata Artist biographies, band member histories, and Wikimedia Commons photo pickers.
  • LRCLIB Automatic synced lyrics lookup for songs without embedded lyrics, plus optional sharing of your timed edits.
2 · Provide Your Own Key Your free personal token · Keychain

Bring your own API keys

Create your own free personal token with the service and paste it into Settings. Keys stay strictly inside your system Keychain.

  • Discogs Deep pressing and edition lookup plus high-resolution release cover candidates.
  • AcoustID Identify mystery songs by how they sound. Computes a fingerprint locally and never uploads audio.
  • fanart.tv Extra high-resolution album covers and artist photography.
  • Spotify & Callasso Read-only Spotify playlist import (using your own free Spotify developer Client ID). Nearby concerts via Callasso, an app from the makers of Elysonic, coming soon.
3 · Provide Your Own AI Local ($0) or Cloud API Key

Bring your own AI & MCP

Run open models locally for $0, or paste your own cloud API key (billed directly by your provider within daily caps you set).

  • Ollama & LM Studio (Local) Run local models right on your machine with zero per-token bills and full privacy.
  • Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok & Mistral Paste your own API key for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, or Mistral, assign different models by job, set fallback order, and cap daily background use.
  • Local Vocal & Whisper Models + MCP Optional on-device lyric alignment models, plus a local MCP server to connect Claude Desktop and other AI clients.
Why does bringing your own APIs matter? Because we never sit in the middle. Elysonic runs no proxy servers, charges no subscription, marks up zero AI tokens, and never touches your keys outside your Keychain. You unlock as much power as you want, and you still pay us $0.

Unlocked · Album Journeys

Hear the story behind every era you own.

Requires Full Discography MusicBrainz Wikipedia Your Own AI

Enable online lookup and connect your own local or cloud AI, and Album Journeys turns a complete studio discography into an interactive, narrated documentary played straight from your own files.

Journeys build quietly in the background and appear once every chapter is finished. Each chapter pairs source-backed writing, credits, related events, and band photography with a synchronized timeline of narration and tracks from your library. MusicBrainz · Discogs · AcoustID · fanart.tv

Edition lookup, audio fingerprinting & batch covers

Turn on online lookup in Settings › Online services to expand Flagged, Tags, and the Artwork Desk:

  • Match exact pressings and editions via MusicBrainz and Discogs, or paste a release link directly
  • Fingerprint untagged tracks locally with AcoustID (your audio file never uploads)
  • Compare Cover Art Archive, Discogs, and fanart.tv covers side by side and fix up to 100 missing albums per batch
  • Run artist-wide Check Albums audits for missing tracks, wrong numbers, and release gaps
Local AI & Frontier Models

In-app AI assistant, genre tagging & playlist picks

Connect a local model (Ollama or LM Studio) or paste your own API key for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, or Mistral in Settings › Assistants:

  • Chat with your collection, queue songs, build playlists, and ask for library cleanups
  • Reads and playback run immediately; every library, tag, or file edit asks Yes/No first
  • Classify missing genres and moods in batches, and get MusicBrainz-verified playlist suggestions
  • Assign different models per job, set fallback order, and cap daily background usage
Local MCP Server · Spotify Client ID

Connect Claude Desktop & import Spotify lists

Turn on the bundled local MCP server in Settings › Other apps to connect Claude Desktop or other AI clients under granular permission tiers and a live activity log. Want to rescue old playlists from Spotify? Supply your own free Spotify developer Client ID to import playlists read-only and match them to your local files.
