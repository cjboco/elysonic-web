EQ Inheritance

Overrides that cascade the way you think

Save a complete EQ curve for a single song, a whole album, a specific DAC or headphone output, or all music. Playback checks from most specific to broadest, and even a saved flat override takes precedence. Remove an override and the next layer takes over.

→ → →

Active rule: Song EQ overrides everything below it, even when you save a flat curve for a bright mastering.

Even Volume

EBU R128 loudness that respects albums

Choose Off, Same for every song, Albums as made, or Automatic. Automatic preserves quiet-to-loud pacing when you play an album in order, then switches to track leveling on shuffle. Dial targets from -23 to -14 LUFS, respect ReplayGain tags, and guard peaks with a true-peak limiter.

Headphones & Outputs

AutoEq profiles, Crossfeed & Setups

Import an AutoEq or Equalizer APO ParametricEQ.txt profile per output, applied before your listening EQ. Soften harsh hard-panned stereo with Light, Medium, or Strong headphone blend, adjust per-output L/R balance, and recall complete Listening Setups via ⌘K.

Sound Path & Mini Player

See every stage of your audio signal

Open Sound Path in Advanced EQ to verify active output negotiation, format conversion, correction, loudness, and EQ in real time. Or pop out the Keep-on-Top Mini Player with seek strip, favorite heart, and live EQ backdrop.

Act V · The Collector's Workshop

Fix decades of messy folders without fear.

Every long-running music library accumulates duplicates, split compilations, missing covers, and spelling drift. Elysonic gives you purpose-built repair desks with previews, safety snapshots, and Undo.

Flagged: every library problem in one place

No more hunting through thousands of folders. Flagged gathers six concrete categories of library drift, lets you audition copies side by side, logs every fix in Changes, and supports Undo.

01

Songs you have twice

Compare bitrates and formats, preview audio, keep the best master, or set extra copies aside.

02

Songs with another name online

Review AcoustID fingerprints and MusicBrainz matches before accepting or declining a title.

03

Artists spelled more than one way

Merge inconsistent spellings in one click or mark intentional variants to stay separate.

04

Songs whose files are gone

Reconnect a moved folder or file, switch to a known copy, or clean up dead entries.

05

Songs whose files kept old details

Inspect failed or skipped file tag writes, retry cleanly, or keep edits library-only.

06

Songs that won't play

Surface corrupt or unreadable audio files to reveal in Finder or move safely to Trash.

Non-destructive edits, release audits & safe file writes

Library corrections persist across rescans without mutating your audio files until you ask. When you are ready to write tags to disk, Elysonic previews an exact write plan first.

Album Lookup & Identify

Match exact editions via MusicBrainz, Discogs, and local AcoustID acoustic fingerprinting. Audition tracks, inspect missing or repeated track numbers, paste release links directly, or run an artist-wide Check Albums report.

Advanced Editing & Classical Works

Lock individual fields against overwrite, revert values to source tags, edit full production credits and mix info, rename discs, and group classical works and movements natively on album pages.

Drag-and-Drop Import & CUE Splitting

Drop any folder onto Elysonic to preview tracks, covers, and optional lookup before copying into a music root. Split whole-disc CUE albums into individual songs, or Smart Replace an existing release while keeping ratings and play history.

Multi-Disc Grouping & Join Albums

Logically join split discs (or split them back apart) with individual disc headers and covers. Or physically consolidate scattered compilation tracks with hard-link safety, crash recovery, and intact song IDs.

Artwork Desk & multi-disc box sets

Filter your entire collection by Missing, Failed, Needs review, Has artwork, or No art. Verify actual cover-file coverage on disk and fix up to 100 albums in a single cancelable batch.

Side-by-side candidate review

Compare embedded images, folder scans, Cover Art Archive releases, Discogs, and fanart.tv matches with full resolution, source, and release details before picking Use this cover. Drop in JPEG, PNG, or WebP files anytime.

Box sets & per-disc covers

Assign dedicated artwork to every disc inside a multi-disc box set, then display either the box's main cover or an automatic collage built from its individual disc covers.

Daily snapshots, encrypted offsite copies & portable Mac transfer

Your database, chosen artwork, playlists, listening history, and Album Journeys are backed up automatically every day with zero cloud subscription.

Compare before restore

Daily local rotation keeps 7 copies with shared artwork storage. Compare dates and song counts against your live library before restoring. Every restore automatically creates a "Before restore" safety copy and recovers cleanly if interrupted.

Encrypted offsite rotation

Point Backups elsewhere at an external drive, NAS, iCloud Drive, or Dropbox folder. Elysonic encrypts and verifies every snapshot, keeps up to 30 copies, and unlocks on a new Mac with your saved recovery code.

Portable .elysonic archives

Move your entire curated library to another Mac in one archive. Remap previous music folder roots to their new paths on import. Provider credentials stay safely in macOS Keychain.

Honest storage boundaries

Library backups protect your Elysonic catalog, artwork, and stories cleanly and fast. Keep your original audio files backed up with Time Machine or your own drive strategy.
